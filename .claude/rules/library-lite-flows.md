## 1 Who uses it (`39:284`)
School admin (registered the school and is the billing contact, handled outside the app; then stays as staff without a class), class teachers, other staff (invited as staff without a class, listed with the teachers), and the Library Lite team (approves schools; a school that's turned down hears why by email after a conversation, outside the app). Any staff member can be made an admin; a school can have several and always keeps one. Only admins make or remove admins, archive or restore teachers and run End of year. Anyone with a login can loan to any child. Children and parents don't log in.

## 2 Site map (`39:311`)
Public website (Home, About, Pricing, Contact) branches to:
- **Register school** (public) → find your school (name or postcode, GIAS list, England) → is this your school? (pick the match; already registered → ask your admin; not listed → type it in) → contacts (name, email, phone) → starting email address (the contact email or a different one, nothing pre-picked) → Library Lite approves by hand (until then the person sees "Thanks, we've got your details") → starting email gets a unique link (works for 7 days).
- **Log in** (public; Forgot password: email → reset link that works once for 12 hours → new password) → Scan Book; Books (search the whole library, filter and sort; a book's page: edit it, its copies, mark a copy lost or damaged, archive the book); Classes (own class only: class list with each child's name showing the colour of their oldest loan and a Quick view of their loans with Return, weekly library day, loan limit, add children by first name + optional initial, one at a time or by pasting a list checked for duplicates; staff without a class are offered: choose a class to view, or scan a book). Tapping a name opens the **Child view**: books on loan now (colour-coded, each with Return after a confirm), borrowing history, move the child to another class (loans go with them), or archive them once they've returned all their books.
- **Log in** also → **Teachers** (any staff member, except where it says admins only): invite a teacher for a class or staff without a class (invite link lasts 14 days; resend or cancel); rename a class, change its teacher or add a second teacher for a job share, from the class page (saves on Save); admins only: archive or restore a teacher (a teacher with a class needs someone to take over the class first), make or remove admins; add or archive classes (blocked while any child has a book out; otherwise archiving also archives its children and history, and a warning asks to confirm or rename instead); Archive (restore any archived class, child or book; teachers by admins only; archived children are deleted for good after 30 days) and Activity log; admins only: End of year; Lost books report and Stats (on-time returns by class or whole school, teachers active each week) for any staff. Invites show as Pending until accepted; after setup the admin lands here.
- **Log in** also → **Help**: quick answers (scanning, barcode or camera problems, loan colours, why a child can't borrow) and a contact line.
- **Log in** also → **My account**: change your own name, email (only after its confirm link; the old address is told), password and the 4-digit PIN for switching user.
- **Log in** also → **Switch user** (shared iPads): tap your name (only staff with a PIN are listed; anyone else can get an email reminder to set one up), enter your PIN (5 wrong → log in with email). A signed-in iPad logs out after 30 minutes without use.

## 3 Onboarding (`40:465`)
School registers (then sees "we've got your details") → Library Lite approves by hand? (No → school doesn't go live; Library Lite talks to them, then emails why, outside the app) → starting email gets unique link (7 days) → school admin creates their account (same password rules) → admin sets up classes with teacher emails (saves as they go; invites only sent on Send invites) → each teacher gets an invite link (lasts 14 days) → teacher creates a password (strength meter + rules: 10+ characters, a number, a special character, not a common password; can't continue until all are met) and sets up class and children, typed or pasted (saves as they go) → class ready.

## 4 Scan flow (`41:388`)
Tap Scan → camera access allowed? (No → remind them to allow it → try again) → scan the barcode (camera or a USB/Bluetooth scanner), or type the ISBN if it won't scan. No barcode → find it by title; not there → add it without a barcode (similar titles are suggested: another copy, or a different book?) → what state is the book in? (A written-off book that's scanned asks whether to add it back.)
- **Not in the library:** barcode recognised and book found? (No → ask staff to scan it again → found this time? Yes → add to library; No → book saved straight away with its barcode → type title + author → attached to the barcode so it's recognised next time; Cancel deletes the saved book and barcode) → add to library → choose Fiction or Non-fiction → loan it now? (No → just added; Yes → loan branch).
- **Available:** loan it → does the person have a class? (Yes → their class is chosen → choose child, with a Change class option; No → choose class → choose child) → at loan limit? (Yes → loan not allowed, no warning; staff can tap the child to see the books they have out) → loan saved with today's date, starts green → on loan.
- **On loan:** return (loan closed → back in library; if several children have the title, staff are asked who's returning it), renew (restarts from today, back to green → child keeps it another week), or **different copy** (loan it straight away under the same title and send the holding child's teacher a copy check, which pops up next time they use the app, or is asked on the spot if it's them: Yes → a second copy; No → the first loan closes as returned).
Every loan, return and renew ends on a confirmation screen.

## 5 Loan rules (`41:549`)
Books are due back on the class's library day: green until the next library day, orange (late) once one library day passes without a return, red after two or more. Limit 2 by default (teacher can change). Renew resets to green, due the next library day. A loan made on any day is due the next library day; changing the library day applies to books already out; a child who moves class follows the new class's day; a class with no library day uses the loan's own weekday. Holidays still count. Weekly reminder email the day before library day. Books belong to the whole school.

## 6 End of the school year (`43:492`)
Loan history stays with a child as their class moves up. Decided: a guided End of year flow, run by an admin from Teachers: 1 new class names (suggested by adding one to the number; staff check each, change the teacher, or mark the class as leaving) → 2 leavers (books still out are each returned or written off by a person; can't finish until all are done) → 3 check and confirm (Move everyone up applies it all in one step, recorded in the activity log) → 4 done (add next year's new class; Lost books report per school year, downloadable). Nothing is written off automatically; a written-off book that turns up can be added back.

## 7 Decisions & out of scope (`41:580`)
Nineteen decisions (Oct 13–15): 1 camera & ISBN, 2 loan limit, 3 search, 4 category, 5 school admin and admin rights, 6 new school year, 7 passwords, 8 copies, 9 never assume, 10 nothing is deleted, 11 lost or damaged, 12 confirmations (no toasts, undo or scan feed), 13 children's names (first name + optional initial), 14 email changes need the confirm link, 15 school approval (no in-app queue), 16 starting email (contact email or a different one), 17 shared iPads (30-minute logout, Switch user with a PIN, no lock screen), 18 archiving a teacher with a class, 19 data & time (keep counting in holidays, any staff sees history, archived children deleted after 30 days, UK time). Plus the out-of-scope list.

## 8 Empty, error and loading states (`82:482`)
Wireframe section 15 States; each code matches the screen it belongs to.
- **Empty:** D1e Books: empty library (→ Scan your first book); E1n Classes: no children yet (add a child or paste a list); E3n Child view: nothing borrowed; D2f Book: never borrowed; G10e Archive: empty; G11e Activity log: empty; K5e Lost books: none; N1e Stats: not enough loans yet (until 10 loans have come back).
- **No results & loading:** D1n Books and C7n Choose child (Clear search); C12n Find by title (still offers adding without a barcode); C1l Finding this book (no answer → type title and author).
- **Errors:** C1x No connection (banner on every page, scanning and saving pause, nothing queued); A3x School lookup not working (type details instead); E1x Didn't save (what was typed stays); X1 Link expired (one shared page for every kind of link); A2x email and password don't match (never says which); A2y account archived.
- **Session:** M1b shared iPad and A2t phone, both logged out after 30 minutes without use.
